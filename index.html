In the classroom I design lessons for mixed groups: visual, spoken and hands-on routes into the same idea, so that every student has a way in. I treat mistakes as evidence of thinking, and I aim for students who can explain why a method works, not only apply it.

How I teach

Understand first, then practise

Students learn why a method works before drilling it. I ask for explanations, not only answers, so that knowledge lasts beyond the test.

Every learner has a way in

I plan differentiated tasks that serve visual, auditory and kinesthetic learners, with support and extension built into the same lesson.

Mistakes are useful

Errors are discussed openly and used to find the next step. Effort and persistence are recognised, so students see ability as something they can grow.

Mathematics connected to the world

Finance, networks, data and engineering problems show students where the ideas are used, drawing on my years in industry.

Learning together

Pair work, peer teaching and structured discussion build communication skills and deepen understanding.

Technology with a purpose

Desmos and GeoGebra make functions and geometry visible and testable. I choose a tool only when it helps students see something they could not see otherwise.
